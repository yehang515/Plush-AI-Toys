## OEM and ODM for AI plush toy brands

Whether you have a complete specification or an early concept, an AI plush project needs both soft-toy craftsmanship and a clear interaction direction. Pluzy supports product conversations from character and material choices through feature planning, samples and packaging.

## OEM: manufacture a defined product

OEM is suited to brands with an established concept, artwork or specification. Bring your requirements for character shape, fabrics, branding, packaging and target market so the product scope can be reviewed accurately.

## ODM: develop from a product brief

ODM is useful when a brand wants help shaping the product before specifications are final. Discuss the character, intended user experience and commercial goals first, then develop a practical product direction.
