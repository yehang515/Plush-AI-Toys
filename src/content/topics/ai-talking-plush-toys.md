## A voice-led plush experience starts with the character

Talking plush products work best when the character and interaction belong together. A friendly voice, a clear role and an appropriate physical design can make the experience easier for users to understand and remember.

## Plan the interaction before production

Define what the toy should do, who it is for and how it should sound. Voice, sound, lights and connected features should be evaluated against the desired user experience, target market and product requirements.
